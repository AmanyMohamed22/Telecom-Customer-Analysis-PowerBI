# 📞 Telecom Customer Churn & Retention Analysis | Digilians

An end-to-end Data Analytics project examining customer churn patterns, revenue exposure, and service behavior for a telecom provider.

![Telecom Dashboard](https://github.com/AmanyMohamed22/Telecom-Customer-Analysis-PowerBI/blob/main/Telecom.png)

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project analyzes **7,043 customers** to identify key churn drivers and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention and protect revenue.

### 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Customers:** 7,043
* **Total Revenue Generated:** $21M
* **Average Tenure:** 32.39 Months
* **Average Data Downloaded:** 176.54K GB

---

## 📊 Key Findings & Insights

1. **Churn Overview:**
   * **Churn Rate:** **26.54%** (1.87K customers) of the overall customer base has churned, representing a substantial loss in recurring revenue.
   * **Active Base:** **67.02%** (4.72K customers) stayed, while **6.45%** (0.45K) are newly joined.

2. **Contract Type & Risk:**
   * **Month-to-Month contracts** show the highest customer churn by a significant margin compared to 1-year and 2-year contracts.
   * Long-term contracts (1 & 2 Years) heavily improve retention and lower drop-off rates.

3. **Pricing & Spending Patterns:**
   * Customers who churned had higher average monthly charges compared to active and newly joined customers.

4. **Payment Preferences:**
   * **Bank Withdrawal** is the most dominant payment method, yet it also holds the highest volume of churned users.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

* **Contract Migration Incentives:** Offer discounts or bundled perks to transition high-risk Month-to-Month customers into 1-Year or 2-Year plans.
* **Targeted Retention for High-Spend Users:** Create tailored loyalty packages or proactive customer service outreach for accounts with high monthly charges.
* **Onboarding Optimization:** Improve the early user experience for newly joined customers (first 6–12 months) to reduce early churn rates.
